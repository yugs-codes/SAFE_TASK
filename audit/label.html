Audit labelling

Labels save straight into audit/*.csv as you go. Press Score when done; results go to audit/results.md.

How to label (decide the edge cases once, then apply them the same way every time)
  • Relevant (y) = a state government body (department, minister, CM, cabinet, police, state agency/PSU, state-run hospital or university, district administration) acts on AI with a commitment: a policy, money, an institution, a deployment, an agreement or a rule, in that state.
  • Not relevant (n): private company news, central schemes with no state partner, speeches or opinions with no commitment, AI mentioned in passing, an individual deepfake crime case.
  • Your calls, pick once: municipal corporations · AI summits hosted by a state · High Court AI guidelines. Write your choice in the first row's note.
  • State correct: the state shown is the government that acts. Category correct: the label is a reasonable fit (the first rule that matched).
  • Dropped items: y means it should have been kept (a miss). Duplicate pairs: y means both headlines report the same announcement.
  • Known events: list events you know happened (2 per state, from your own reading or official sites), then search the records and mark found y/n.
  • Keys: Y / N fill the next empty label in the highlighted row, ↓↑ or JK move, U clears the row, O opens the article.
