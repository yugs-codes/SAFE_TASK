    Records

    Method

    Sources

    News events come from Google News RSS searches (6 query variants per focus state, run once per quarter so each slice gets its own ~100-item budget). No article text is fetched, so labels and summaries come from the headline.

    Policy instruments come from a research snapshot of Acts, policies, missions and guidelines compiled from official sources and press reports (provenance in the README). Union-level records, court instruments and AI-adjacent ones (data-centre, gig-work, general IT policy) are excluded.

    Pipeline
    1. Collect Google News RSS items for each state, query and quarter.
    2. Filter: keep a headline only if it has an AI term and a state-government signal; drop private-sector and central-government-only news. Every drop keeps its reason.
    3. Normalize: the state comes from the headline, not the query (UP, U.P., Uttar-Pradesh and उत्तर प्रदेश are one state); dates become IST calendar days.
    4. Deduplicate: same state, within 3 days, similar distinctive words; different companies or proposed-vs-done never merge.
    5. Classify category and stage by keyword rules, first match wins.
    Confidence

    Confidence measures how well the coverage is corroborated, not whether it is true. High: an official source or 3+ outlets. Medium: a major outlet or 2 outlets. Low: one outlet. Every record carries a note saying why.

    Dates

    News dates are publication dates in IST, the earliest in each cluster; they approximate, but are not, event dates. Instrument dates are notification or approval dates, shown at day, month or year precision as documented.

    Known holes

    English headlines only (one Hindi query for Uttar Pradesh); about 100 results per query; no official state portals in the news layer; syndicated wire copy inflates outlet counts; stage is keyword-based. The README lists every known hole and the audited precision.
